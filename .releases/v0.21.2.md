### What changes since `0.21.1`

- Fixed: the bounds of a plot are no longer drawn in the colour of the first curve with Makie. The bound and time lines take the concrete colours of `CTBase.Plotting.COLORS`; nothing changes with Plots (#527, #528).
- Dependencies: the compat of `CTBase` moves to `0.30.14`, the release that provides `COLORS`.
- Documentation: the pins of ct-conventions and ct-agents, the docs build command, the preview label (#517, #523, #524).
- Breaking: none (a `Non-breaking note (0.21.2)` goes in `BREAKING.md`).
- Not in `[Unreleased]` yet, added by the release PR: none. The candidates listed by `facts` are two formatter runs (#514, #522), the pin of ct-conventions (#524) and the compat bump, all already covered.
- Open PRs, not in this release: #489 CompatHelper: bump compat for JET in [extras] to 0.13 (draft).
